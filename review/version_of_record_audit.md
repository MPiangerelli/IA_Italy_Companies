# Version-of-record audit of the academic layer (4 October 2026)

Inclusion criterion (decided 3-4 October 2026): journal articles must be published in their version of record (online or in an issue) by 3 October 2026; accepted manuscripts, "forthcoming" articles and journal pre-proofs are excluded. Working papers are grey literature and are not subject to the criterion, but they are no longer counted as peer-reviewed.

Status of the 109 documents of the academic layer (Crossref metadata checked on 4 October 2026; per-record table in `version_of_record_audit.csv`):

| Status | n |
|---|---|
| Version of record in an issue (volume, issue or article number) | 97 |
| Version of record online first, issue not yet assigned (Crespi et al. 2026, Cattaruzzo et al. 2026, Horvat et al. 2026; Taylor & Francis "published online") | 3 |
| Books and book chapters (Tornatzky and Fleischer 1990, Rogers et al. 2009, Baker 2012) | 3 |
| Working papers (Bratta et al. 2020, Calvino and Fontanelli 2023, Cullen et al. 2025, Ferrando et al. 2026, Cette et al. 2026, Tyndall 2010) | 6 |

No accepted manuscript or pre-proof is in the corpus. Excluded under the criterion: Garlatti Costa et al. (IJVCM, forthcoming) and Forgione and Migliardo 2026 (IJIS, journal pre-proof available online since July 2026; to be re-assessed if a version of record appears).
